Backup Yamaha DX7 Patches Without Installing Software

The Yamaha DX7 was released in 1983. Its microprocessors expect strict SysEx parameters. If you send data too quickly or without disabling internal protection, the system ignores the incoming buffer. This guide shows you how to configure the physical interface and memory protect registers on the DX7 to dump presets directly to your browser tab.

Hardware Setup Steps

  • Step 1: Press FUNCTION, then press button 8.
  • Step 2: Press button 8 repeatedly until the screen reads MIDI CH = 1.
  • Step 3: Press FUNCTION, then press button 11.
  • Step 4: Press 11 repeatedly until the screen reads SYS INFO IR. Change this to SYS INFO AV (Available) using the YES key.
  • Step 5: Press INTERNAL, then press MEMORY PROTECT (button 14).
  • Step 6: Press 14 again to switch the screen from ON to OFF using the NO key.

Remember this

If your DX7 screen freezes during a dump, you are likely using a cheap USB-MIDI cable. These cables lack optocouplers and drop bytes when transmitting SysEx packets larger than 256 bytes.

Ready to backup?

Now that your hardware is configured, connect your MIDI cables and backup your DX7 patches directly to the cloud.
