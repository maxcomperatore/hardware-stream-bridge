Our Blueprint

The Product Vision
& Roadmap

Building the default operating system for vintage gear. No driver directories, no floppy disks, no legacy software downloads. Just the web.

I. The Product Pillars

01

Universal Support

Support for over 50 classic synths (including the Roland D-50, Casio CZ series, Yamaha TX81Z, E-mu Proteus, Korg Wavestation, and more). One console to rule them all.

02

Clean database

Say goodbye to searching shady, dead 90s websites for corrupted .syx soundbanks. knob.monster hosts a secure, crowdsourced digital repository of clean patches.

03

Stage Integration

Designed for active performers. Musicians use Web MIDI on their iPads on stage via Bluetooth MIDI or USB-C adapters to load presets in real-time between songs.

II. How We Keep It Running

No corporate bloat. Just direct utility.

We do not sell ads, we do not track your behavior, and we do not lock your data. You get a secure, fast vault for your classic synthesizers, and we get the resources to keep the servers online.

Synthesizers built in the 80s and 90s are physical art, but their memory is fragile. Backup batteries die, storage gets corrupted, and original soundbanks get lost. We built knob.monster to make patch backup and recovery as simple as plugging in a USB cable.

A Win-Win Transaction

Your knob.monster subscription gives you instant cloud recall and stage-ready iPad transfers. In return, your funding keeps the platform fast, secure, and continuously updated with new synth profiles. Win-win.
