Vintage pedalboard gear are incredible instruments, but their internal memory is surprisingly fragile. Here are 3 facts every synth owner should know:

🔋 1. The Internal Battery Clock

Classic pedals like the Yamaha DX7, Korg M1, and Roland Juno-106 store your custom soundbanks in volatile RAM powered by a tiny internal lithium battery (CR2032). When that battery dies, all custom soundbanks vanish permanently upon power off.

🔍 2. SysEx Header Signatures

Every SysEx dump starts with F0 and ends with F7. In between sits the manufacturer ID (e.g. 43 for Yamaha, 41 for Roland). Rigluk uses these exact byte signatures to auto-detect your synth in milliseconds.

⚡ 3. Instant Web MIDI Vaults

You don't need old MIDI-OX software or serial drivers anymore. Modern Web MIDI lets you back up your guitar pedal & amp modeler directly inside Chrome or Edge with zero latency.
