FDA Draft Guidance Raises Evidentiary Bar for Flavored Vape PMTAs

The FDA published Draft Guidance Docket #2026-04732, outlining the agency's non-binding current thinking on evidentiary standards for flavored ENDS PMTAs under Section 910 of the FD&C Act. The document signals intensified scrutiny of the Appropriate for the Protection of Public Health standard, requiring applicants to quantitatively demonstrate that adult smoker conversion benefits decisively outweigh youth initiation risks. While failure to adopt the guidance does not automatically trigger statutory Civil Money Penalties, non-compliance materially elevates the probability of a Refuse to File or Marketing Denial Order, establishing a de facto litigation-proof standard for portfolio defense.
