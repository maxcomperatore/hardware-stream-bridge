Battery Health Advisory

Vintage internal static RAM batteries degrade silently over time.

Protect your custom soundbank library

Synthesizers like the Roland Juno-106, Yamaha DX7, Korg M1, and Oberheim Matrix-1000 store factory presets and custom patches in battery-backed SRAM.

When these internal CR2032 or soldered coin batteries drop below 2.5V, custom soundbanks and parameter tweaks are permanently wiped.
