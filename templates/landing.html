Pure Fidelity. Timeless Memory.

Not merely a backup. A physical preservation of sonic soul.

Every patch, filter curve, and analog nuance is captured with absolute reverence. Our SysEx engine records standard MIDI dumps with byte-for-byte precision, capturing every parameter exactly as it emerged from your synthesizer’s microprocessor. Calibrated data pacing honors the delicate architectural pulse of vintage 8-bit chips, guaranteeing your sonic identity remains forever untouched, untamed, and instantly recalled.

Abstracting 40 years of hardware friction.

The MIDI Handshake

Plug in your synth using a standard USB-to-MIDI adapter. Open bipluk: the browser Web MIDI API initiates a handshake immediately.

Index Extraction

We send specific low-level request sequences to dump presets. The parser instantly extracts text characters from the registers in real-time.

Single-Click Recall

Hit Sync to write sound banks back. We flash the entire hexadecimal data payload, overwriting hardware RAM registers instantly.
